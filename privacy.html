Privacy notice

How the school handles information submitted through this website.

Information collected

When you submit an enquiry, the site stores your name, contact details, enquiry topic, and message. An application request also stores your selected programme and a summary of your previous education. Do not include national identification numbers, financial information, health information, or document scans.

Why information is used

The school uses these details to respond to enquiries and follow up on application requests. An application request is not a final admission decision or a substitute for any official application documents.

Storage and access

Submitted details are stored in the website's database on the server hosting the site. Authorized administrators can view and delete submissions through a password-protected page. The school should restrict server access, use HTTPS, protect database backups, and review its retention practices.

The system does not automatically delete records. School administrators should delete information that is no longer needed, in line with the school's approved retention policy.

WhatsApp

After a successful form submission, you may choose to open WhatsApp with a prepared message. The message is not sent until you review it and press Send. If you choose to send it, WhatsApp handles that message under its own privacy terms.

Your choices

You may choose not to submit a form. To ask the school to review or delete a submission, contact it through WhatsApp and identify the submission you want reviewed.

The school should review this notice and confirm its contact and data-retention procedures before the website is used to collect real applicant information.
