Application process

Our administration team can guide you through the current intake and application requirements.

1

Choose a programme

Review the available programmes and identify the one that matches your training and career plans.

2

Confirm requirements

Ask the school about the entry criteria, required documents, application form, fees, and intake dates.

3

Prepare your application

Gather the requested documents and follow the submission instructions provided by the administration team.

4

Follow up

Check with the school about your application status and any next steps for the selected intake.

Send an application request

Share your details and the school can follow up about the selected programme. This request is not a final admission decision or a substitute for official application documents.

What to prepare

Document requirements can differ by programme and intake. Contact the school to confirm the official checklist before submitting an application.

  • Academic certificates or result documents
  • Identification document
  • Recent passport-size photographs
  • Completed application form, as directed by the school

Questions about applying?

Contact the school administration for current intake dates, programme entry requirements, application forms, and the latest fee structure.
